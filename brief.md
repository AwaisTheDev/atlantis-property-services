Brief description of your business: Atlantis Facilities Management is a locally based property services business focused on Melbourne's Suburbs. It provides customers with a simple, single point of contact for coordinating residential and commercial property services. Initial services include gardening and landscaping, pressure washing, window cleaning, painting, fence repairs, residential cleaning, deep cleaning, builders cleans, rubbish removal and handyman services. Services are fulfilled through an approved Atlantis service-partner network, but Atlantis remains the customer-facing relationship and the partner operating model stays behind the scenes. 
2. Business Goals 
Primary purpose of website: Generate customer job requests, quote enquiries and service booking requests, including urgent/emergency requests. The customer experience should feel like dealing directly with one managed property-services company, not an open marketplace. 
Target audience: Residential property owners, tenants, landlords/property investors, property managers and commercial/business customers. 
What makes the business unique: One convenient point of contact across multiple property services. Atlantis receives the request, coordinates suitable providers, manages the quote/service process and remains accountable for the customer experience. 
Core model: Customer -> Atlantis -> approved partner network -> Atlantis-managed quote/service -> customer. 
3. Website Content & Customer Journey 
Required pages / sections: Home; Services (all services on one page); FAQs; Become a Service Partner; Contact information; Privacy Policy; Website Terms; customer job/quote request form; booking request pathway; Emergency/Urgent Service pathway; Thank-you/confirmation page. No dedicated About Us page at launch.

Homepage primary CTA: Use customer-friendly wording such as "Tell Us What You Need" as the primary job-posting/request pathway, with "Request a Quote" able to remain as supporting language where useful. Keep Book a Service and Emergency/Urgent Service clearly accessible. 
How Atlantis Works: 1. Tell us what you need. 2. We arrange it. 3. We get it done. 
Customer experience: The form should feel like a fast, modern job-posting flow rather than a generic contact form. Aim for a simple mobile-first experience that can be completed quickly, with conditional questions rather than unnecessary fields on one screen. 
Why Choose Atlantis: Convenience, simplicity, local focus, one point of contact, access to multiple property services and coordinated service delivery, insured and vetted network of service providers.

4. Design Preferences 
Preferred style / look: Concept attached below. Use more light colours overall or where possible.
Avoid: Overly dark/heavy layouts, clutter, complicated navigation, excessive pop-ups, flashy effects, unnecessary animation, public provider comparison screens or anything that weakens Atlantis as the primary brand. 
Accessibility: Follow standard modern web-accessibility best practices. 
Mobile-first: Very important. CTAs, forms, uploads and tap-to-call functionality should work especially well on mobile. 
Navigation / CTA: Keep navigation simple. 
Local positioning: Describe Atlantis as locally based and focused on Melbourne's inner suburbs, initially servicing approximately 15 km from Melbourne CBD. Avoid "locally owned" or other ownership claims.

Preferred launch date: ASAP.

6. Become a Service Partner Seperate TAB
Navigation: Include "Become a Service Partner" in the main navigation. 
Page positioning: Briefly explain the opportunity to receive suitable work through Atlantis and build an ongoing working relationship, without promising job volumes. Applications are subject to review and approval. 
Application fields: Contact name; business name; business structure; ABN; mobile; email; services/trades offered; general geographic service area; years in business/operating experience; availability for one-off and recurring work; urgent/emergency availability; typical response time; general days and usual working hours. 
Licences / insurance: Ask whether relevant licences and appropriate insurance are held. Require applicable licences/certificates of currency or insurance documents at application. 
Verification / declarations: Applicant agrees Atlantis may verify ABN, licences and insurance; declares information/documents current and accurate; consents to contact about the application and future service opportunities.
 Approval conditions: Atlantis may request additional information/documents. Client references are not required initially but may be requested later. Approval does not guarantee minimum work volume/frequency. Approved partners must accept separate Atlantis Service Partner Terms before onboarding is finalised. 
Future partner-platform expectation: Approved partners may later receive suitable job opportunities and submit availability/pricing through a private Atlantis system. This is an internal partner workflow; partners are not publicly presented as competing providers to Atlantis customers. 
Partner terms: Separate from public website Terms & Conditions.
